Frequently asked
questions.

Do you only work with real estate?

No. BPRE Visuals works with property owners, real estate professionals, developers, businesses, entrepreneurs and brands.

Do you publish your prices online?

We don't publish fixed prices because every project can have different requirements. Contact us with your project details and we'll discuss the appropriate solution.

Can you create recurring content?

Yes. We can discuss ongoing content solutions for businesses and brands that need consistent photography and video.

Do you work with real estate developers?

Yes. We can create photography, video and promotional content for developments, properties and construction projects.

Where do you operate?

BPRE Visuals is based in Curaçao. We primarily serve clients in Curaçao, with the ambition to work on projects beyond the island as the company grows.
